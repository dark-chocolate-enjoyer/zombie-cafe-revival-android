  1. Café Menu

    These are the buttons in the top right of the main café screen.
    • Tap the Zombie Café logo to return to the Main Menu.
    • Tap Gacha to open the gacha screen.
    • Tap Roulette to open the roulette screen. (It may not always be shown.)
    • Tap Free Toxin to open the free offers screen. (It may not always be shown.)
    • Tap Event to open the event screens.
    These are the buttons in the top left of the main café screen.
    • Tap the shopping bag icon to open the store. You can drag items out of the store to buy them, or drag items from your café back into the store to sell them. Tap Orders to see the orders you currently have.
    • Tap the map icon to view your neighborhood map, where you can raid other cafés and visit friends.
    • Tap Locker to bring stored zombies back to your café or fire them. To put a zombie in the Meat Locker, tap the zombie in your café and then tap the Store button. When the Meat Locker is full, you can spend Toxin to expand it.
    These are the buttons in the tray menu in the bottom right of the café screen.
    • Tap Recipes to open your recipe book. Flick the menu at the top left or right to flip between recipes, including rare/special versions. Tap Cook to choose a dish, then choose a zombie to cook it.
    • Tap the zombie button to view bios of your current staff (and optionally fire them to make room). The number underneath this icon represents how many employees you have, out of your current limit.
    • Tap Rating to open the Review System.
    • Tap Orders to open Orders from HQ.
    • Tap Storage to store furniture from your café.
    • Tap Collection to view the Zombie Collection you've gathered so far.
    When these areas have something new that's of interest, there will be a bouncing exclamation mark above them.

  2. Café Awning

    This is the information displayed at the top of the main café screen.
    • Under "Your Café" is your café's star rating. Your rating indicates your café's current popularity, and will go up or down depending on the consistency of your service, and what items you have in your café. Keep serving food to get a higher rating and more customers. More customers means more money!
    • To the right of "Your Café" is your café's level number and XP (experience) progress bar. Keep cooking and raiding to gain XP - with every level you unlock new recipes, competitors, zombies, and new items in the store!
    • To the right of the XP bar are your current supplies of cash and Toxin. Get more cash by serving and raiding. Get more Toxin by coming back every day, winning raids, or trading cash for Toxin in the store.
    • Tap the Toxin button to open the Toxin screen.

    Touch the door at any time to close the café. Your star rating will slowly drop while it is closed.

  3. Chef Avatar

    If you decide you want to change your chef avatar, select your chef and then press the "chef" button to view all of the available avatars. (This list will be updated periodically.) Zombie chefs will have a higher energy/defense when your friends raid your café, making it harder for them to win. These are roughly equivalent to the strength that each of these zombie types would have as an employee. An exclamation point will appear over the chef button when new avatars are available.

  4. Cooking

    Select a stove to bring up the recipe book and choose a recipe. Cooking and serving can be done by both the chef (your avatar) and any of your undead employees. Select a character and then select the stove you would like them to cook on. To view the time remaining on a dish, touch the stove.

    When your dish is finished cooking, bring it to the serving counter by touching your chef or a zombie, and then touching the stove with the dish. If you don't serve a dish, it will burn on the stove.

    Zombies can cook, but will slowly use up their energy while cooking. A cooking dish needs to be attended to by a zombie or your chef for as long as it takes to finish.

    Tip: Always make sure you have something cooking to ensure you have enough food to serve. Cooking is also the best way to gain XP and level up your café.

  5. Recipes

    You can touch your recipe book or a stove to view your recipes. Recipes vary greatly in their costs and rewards. Some are quick and cheap, and others are expensive and take days to cook - you don't need to wait around and watch, but make sure you've got enough for your customers in the mean time.

    You can get new recipes by leveling up, stealing food from enemy cafés, and ordering from friends. Stolen dishes, dishes made for friends and dishes received from friends are stored in the fridge. Touch the fridge at any time to see its contents. Ordered food in the fridge can also be discarded, if necessary.

    Tip: Stealing food during raids and ordering from friends can unlock rare/special versions of recipes that are often more valuable than their normal counterparts. Each of these special versions have different bonuses, but it's not always obvious. If you have a dish in your fridge that has an UNLOCK button, it means you don't have that recipe - it's wise to UNLOCK it rather than serve it.

  6. Limited Recipes

    Dishes marked "Limited Recipe" in your recipe book are bonus dishes for this version only! They give more XP than normal dishes, so they're an efficient way to level up.

    Note: Limited dishes change into different dishes when the app is updated. They also change into different dishes when sent to friends playing an older or newer version.

  7. Serving

    Select a zombie (or your chef) and touch a serving counter or table to have them serve customers. While they're serving, workers will automatically take dishes to waiting customers and clear dirty dishes.

    You don't need to tell servers what to do, but you need to make sure they've got a clear path to every table, serving counter, and sink. If a server can't reach a table they won't serve customers who sit there.

    Tip: Like cooking, zombies use energy to serve. However, the more servers you have the less work it is for each of them - you'll serve customers faster and use less energy.

  8. Customers

    Customers will visit your café looking for a meal. If they leave happy, they'll give you money and improve your star rating (leading to more customers). If they can't find a clean table to sit at, or wait too long to be served, they'll leave unhappy - lowering your rating.

    Selecting a customer will show you their name. If you have room for another zombie, you can touch the INFECT button to turn them into a zombie. The type of customer you choose to infect will affect how good they are at working in the café.

    Tip: Watch customers (and touch the INFO button to see their bio) to get clues as to their individual strengths and weaknesses. As your café advances different customers will arrive - keep your eyes peeled!

  9. Zombies

    Selecting a zombie will show you their name, energy bar, and their current status (cooking, serving, resting, etc.) If your zombie's energy is low, you can press the REST button to give them a break while their energy recovers. You can also revitalize them instantly by pressing ENERGIZE to spend a vial of toxin.

    While they're selected, touching a stove, table, serving counter or the work button will immediately put them back to work. Selecting the INFO button will bring up your zombie's bio and allow you to fire them. Zombies will gain experience by serving and raiding friends and enemies. When a zombie levels up, its maximum energy will increase. Zombies will not always stay on task or prioritize - sometimes they'll daydream or fall asleep! So be prepared to remind them once in a while what they should be doing. Tired zombies (zombies with low energy) are more likely to make mistakes or try to eat the customers.

    Tap Store to send a zombie to the Meat Locker. Keep your extra zombies in the Meat Locker.

    From the zombie's info screen you can teach it a Zombie Move. You can also relearn a different move later.

    Tip: Try your zombies in different roles to see what works best. Some zombies have more energy, some are stronger, some are faster, some get more tips, some are better at staying on task and not attacking your customers.

  10. Tombstones

    Tombstones are used to give your zombie horde special bonuses, when placed outside your café.

    During a raid, touch a tombstone icon to give all of your zombies that bonus for five seconds. Only one type of bonus can be used at a time, and while that bonus is in effect, the zombies will glow the color of the tombstone. Each standard tombstone can be used a maximum of five times, before requiring 24 hours to regenerate.

  11. Ampoules

    Give ampoules to your zombies to raise their stats.

    Open a zombie's info screen and tap the icons in the "Zombie Ampoules" section to equip or change ampoules. You can hold up to 9 of each type of ampoule; any beyond 9 (for example from raid rewards) are discarded, so equip them on zombies to make room. The number of ampoules a zombie can equip goes up as it levels up, to a maximum of 5.

    An ampoule is used up once you swap it out for a different one. Ampoules can be earned as raid rewards and more.

  12. Sacrifice Kitchen

    Sacrificing zombies raises another zombie's experience.

    Select the zombie you want to power up and a Power Up button appears in the bottom right. Tap it to go to the Sacrifice Kitchen. Tap the zombies you want to sacrifice, then tap Sacrifice to begin. You can sacrifice up to 10 zombies at once. The XP gained depends on the rarity and level of the sacrificed zombies, and a zombie's maximum level depends on its rarity.

    Note: Sacrificed zombies are gone for good.

  13. The Store

    Select the shopping bag icon on the left of the café screen to view store items. Drag left or right, or tap the red arrows, to view items. Touch or drag an item to place it in your café, and press purchase to buy it. You can touch and hold items in your café to move them later, and drag them back into the store to sell them.

    Some of the items will be locked until your café is a higher level.

    • Cash Items
      Items with dollar sign symbols will accumulate cash on their own. A dollar sign or an exclamation point will appear above an item when it has cash available, and tapping it will add some cash to your total. Items with an exclamation point are full and cannot accumulate any more cash until they've been emptied.

    • XP Items
      Items with XP symbols will accumulate XP on their own. An XP symbol or an exclamation point will appear above an item when it has XP available; tap it to collect. Items with an exclamation point are full and cannot accumulate any more XP until they've been collected.

    • Popularity Items
      Items with star symbols will help your star rating increase faster and decrease slower. Like cash items, the more stars, the better.

    • Toxin Barrels
      Toxin barrels help your zombies regain energy faster. You can have more than one barrel to increase the effect.

    • Café Expansions
      Besides items, you can also buy expansions to your café. Adding more room gives you more room to serve and customize. Bigger cafés can make more money, and faster.

  14. Item Storage

    Select the icon in the top left of the café screen to open the item storage tab. Drag an item into the storage tab to store it. Stored items can be dragged back into your café at any time.

  15. Meat Locker

    Select the icon in the top right of the café screen to open the Meat Locker. From here you can bring stored zombies back to your café or fire them.

    To put a zombie in the Meat Locker, tap the zombie in your café and then tap the Store button. When the Meat Locker is full, you can spend Toxin to expand it.

  16. Zombie Moves

    At the Zombie Dojo you can spend Toxin to teach your zombies Zombie Moves.

    Zombie Moves are special actions your zombies use during raids. They have all kinds of effects and give you the upper hand in battle. If you want to change a move a zombie has learned, you can spend Toxin to teach it a new one.

  17. The Map

    Next to your café on the map are your friends' cafés and rival cafés.

    1. Friends/Franchises
      You can invite friends by logging into Facebook from the Main Menu, or by tapping "Invite Friends" next to your café on the map. While in a friend's café, you can collect franchise fees, or order food from their recipe book. You can only order one dish from each friend per day.

      When a friend orders a dish from you, a "!" appears on the clipboard on the left of the screen. Tap Orders to choose whether to accept the order. If a stove is free, you can start cooking. Finished orders are listed under the Done button on the clipboard; tap Deliver to send them. You can also deliver other dishes from your fridge. Dishes you cooked for an order are kept in your fridge with the name of the friend they're for. If you can't deliver a dish or decide to serve it in your own café, tap Use.

      Tip: Ordering food from friends is a great way of unlocking valuable and rare recipes you don't yet have. Filling orders is a great way to get quick cash and share recipes with friends.

      If yours or your friends' cafés appear greenish on the map, it means they're under investigation by the Zombie Labor Union. You won't be able to visit them until the investigation is complete. During an investigation you also can't get the Facebook photo bonus or ranking scores.

    2. Enemy Cafés/Raids
      After selecting a café to raid, select each zombie and tap the Raid button to start the attack. If you want them to attack a specific person, or steal food, tap the zombie and then your target. Tap the retreat button (the white flag in the top left) to have your zombies try to escape.

      If you defeat an enemy café, it will be forced to close, but a new restaurant will open in its place after a while. If an enemy café is allowed to operate in your area for too long, it will start to steal your customers!

      Tip: Raiding enemy cafés is another great way to get valuable and rare recipes. You also have a chance of winning Toxin! Zombies that know Zombie Moves will give you the upper hand in raids.
    3. Boss Fights
      After level 5, boss cafés will occasionally appear on the map. Defeat the chef of one of these cafés to win a rare recipe. The chance of a boss café appearing goes up each time you level up.
    4. Zombie Survival
      After level 8, the Zombie Survival arena (the graveyard) appears on the map. Tap "Let's Survive" to enter. Choose the zombies you want to send in (up to 6) and tap "Send all zombies" to start. The battle continues until all of your zombies are defeated, but you can spend Toxin along the way to revive them, and spend Toxin to continue. Once the arena closes you must wait 2 hours, but you can reopen it with cash.
    5. Raiding Friends
      You can raid a friend's café by tapping Raid while visiting it. As with enemy raids, your zombies can die. Even if you win, your friend loses nothing. A "!" on the clipboard means your café was raided; tap it to see the details.

  18. The Review System

    Tap the zombie head icon in the tray menu to open the Review System.

    Complete the objectives on the review screen one by one to add bonus stars to your café's rating. Bonus stars last longer than normal stars, and you can have up to 3 of them.

    When an objective is complete, it flashes red. Tap a completed objective to receive a bonus star. Objectives come in four types: Cooking, Social, Café, and Raids.

    The 1st bonus star lasts for 48 hours.
    The 2nd bonus star adds 36 hours to the bonus duration.
    The 3rd bonus star adds 24 hours to the bonus duration.

    To keep all 3 bonus stars, complete all of your objectives within 24 hours. At the maximum of 3 stars, every full set of completed objectives extends the bonus by 24 hours.

    Bribe the Reviewer
    Spend Toxin to complete an objective instantly.

  19. Orders from HQ

    Tap the phone icon in the tray menu to open Orders from HQ.

    Complete the orders HQ gives you to receive special bonuses. The more orders you complete, the higher your HQ Rating will climb.

    Some orders even reward Toxin. Complete orders and earn Toxin!

  20. Zombie Collection

    Tap the book icon on the right of the screen to open the Zombie Collection.

    The Zombie Collection shows info on every zombie you've infected or won from the Zombie Roulette. Hire every zombie and aim for 100% completion!

  25. Frequently Asked Questions

    1. How do I add people to my friends list?
      Facebook friends who already play the game are added automatically. You can invite friends from "Friends" in the Main Menu.
    2. Will my café earn money while the app is closed?
      Yes, your café keeps earning while the app is closed. Just make sure you don't run out of food.
    3. How do I move my furniture/appliances/etc?
      Touch and hold an item until it turns green, then drag it to move it. Tap the arrows to change its direction. With the store open, drag an item into the store to sell it. Chairs can also be rotated a full 360 degrees.
    4. How do I sell my furniture/appliances/etc?
      Tap the shopping bag button to open the store. Then touch and hold the item you want to sell until it turns green, and drag it into the store window.
    5. What happens if all of my food gets piled on one serving counter?
      Don't worry! You won't lose any food by stacking it, though you won't be able to see all of it. It's not how things work in real life, but at least you only need one counter.
    6. How long does it take food to burn?
      Food burns if it's left on the stove for as long as it took to cook. (Mystery Meat is the exception - it burns after 5 minutes.)
    7. What items earn extra cash?
      Items in the store with a "$" symbol, like the ATM and arcade games, earn cash. A "!" means the item is full - collect the earnings.
    8. What is the barrel for in special items?
      It shortens the time it takes your zombies to recover energy.
    9. Why is the zombie union rep telling me my zombies are overworked/Why is that café appearing green on the map?
      Your device's date and time settings don't match the server's.
    10. How do I close my café?
      Tap the door to close your café. You can redecorate without worrying about customers, but your star rating will slowly drop while you're closed (not as fast as when you run out of food).
    11. Why is my customer running around screaming/Why is my zombie doing nothing?
      Make sure customers and staff have room to walk. You may be blocking their path.
    12. What happens if I raid my friend's café?
      Don't worry! Your friend won't lose any food, customers, or staff.
