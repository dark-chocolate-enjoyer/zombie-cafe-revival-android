# v1.0 Balance Summary

Technical summary of the Zombie Cafe Revival v1.0 food economy rework. All 216 dishes in the game's food data were extracted, measured, and reviewed; changes were applied in validated stages.

## Balance philosophy

- **Fun over perfect smoothing.** The curve doesn't need to be mathematically flat — it needs to feel good. Some spikes are features.
- **Less grind.** Progression should reward playing, not waiting.
- **Premium investment should matter.** Paid cookbooks, toxin recipes, and the special stove chain should outperform free food, scaling with the investment.
- **Hard boss rewards should remain special.** The best recipes in the game stay locked behind the hardest boss/raid cafes, and their values stay elite.

## Metrics used

Each dish was evaluated on:

- Total earnings (servings × price per serving)
- Total profit (earnings − cost)
- Profit per minute
- XP per minute
- Servings
- Cook time
- Unlock level
- Acquisition type (level unlock, cookbook purchase, quest, etc.)
- Ownership class: free/vanilla, premium cookbook, special stove chain, raid, unknown/promo

## Balance stages

The rework was applied as a sequence of staged, individually validated passes:

1. **Initial cleanup passes** — first corrections to the worst outliers and broken values.
2. **Clean-number cleanup** — normalized awkward values to clean, readable numbers.
3. **Stage F — premium/stove-chain redesign** (41 dishes) — the paid-content economy rework.
4. **Stage E — free/vanilla progression pass** (115 dishes) — tapered profit/XP lifts for free recipes only.
5. **Manual tweak** — Green Eggs & Sam PricePerServing 2 → 3, accepted as an explicit manual v1.0 adjustment.

Every stage was applied by script with preflight current-value checks, value-range safety limits (all fields must stay within the game's 16-bit data format), no-nerf guarantees where promised, anchor-dish verification, and full before/after audits.

## Stage F — premium and stove-chain redesign

- **41 rows**: paid cookbook and special-stove-chain recipes.
- Paid and stove-chain recipes were made more rewarding so that premium investment scales rather than falling behind free food.
- The **Pirate, Vampire, Mafia, Medieval, Super Hero, Politician, and Day of the Dead** themed recipes all gained stronger progression value.

## Stage E — free/vanilla progression pass

- **115 rows**: free/vanilla dishes only.
- Explicitly excluded: anything owned by Stage F (Stage F wins on overlap), other premium cookbook dishes, raid dishes, protected anchor/outlier dishes, unknown/promo dishes, and rows held for later review.
- Applied tapered profit and XP lifts — stronger help where the curve was weakest, lighter touch where it was already healthy.
- During validation, a leaked debug value was found and fixed: **Dishwater Soup** had a 1-minute cook time from a debug fast-track build; it was restored to its intended **15 minutes**.

## Stage I — toxin exchange rebalance

- The cash-to-toxin exchange was raised by **1.5×** to keep premium currency meaningful after the stronger v1.0 food economy.
- New store prices: **10 toxin $30,000 · 30 toxin $75,000 · 175 toxin $375,000 · 750 toxin $1,500,000** (the toxin amounts granted are unchanged).
- The uniform multiplier preserves the original bulk-discount curve and tracks the ~45–55% food-income buff, so the grind-time to afford one toxin stays close to vanilla.
- Data-only change: the four toxin-pack `Price` fields in `furnitureData.bin.mid.json`.

## Overall practical effect (estimate)

These are estimates from the before/after data, not exact proofs:

- The food economy feels roughly **45–55% more rewarding overall**.
- Free/normal progression is roughly **20–40% stronger**.
- Premium/DLC/stove-chain dishes are often **much stronger** and are now genuinely worth investing in.

## Intentionally not finalized in v1.0

- **Full raid pass** — raid/boss recipe values are preserved/provisional; a dedicated pass will follow.
- **Unknown/promo recipes** — a small set of unclear-origin recipes was left untouched pending research.
- **Character/zombie economy** — zombie costs, training, and earnings are out of scope for v1.0.
