**Zombie Cafe Revival v1.1 — Balance + Music Fix**

An unofficial fan balance mod for *Zombie Cafe*. v1.1 keeps the v1.0 food economy rework and adds a full music-state fix plus a complete character rebalance.

## 🎵 Music fix (headline feature)

- Music no longer dies after the first track — it keeps playing for the whole session.
- Your cafe now rotates through the main cafe tracks: **Zombie Theme V1**, **Zombie Mini Cue 2g**, **Zombie Mini Cue 3g**.
- Cafe music resumes correctly after returning from the map, a raid, or any cafe reload (the case that used to go silent).
- The map screen stays silent on purpose.
- Raid music starts when you pick a raid cafe — not the moment you open the map.
- Normal raids play **Mini Cue 1g / Mini Cue 1V2** (random); the **Haunted House** theme plays for the Halloween/special boss cafe.
- Victory sting now uses **Zombie Sting 2g**.
- Feels less spammy: a short pause before cafe music restarts on entry, and a longer quiet gap after a track ends before the next one fades in.

## ⚖️ Character balance

- **Playable chefs** rebalanced so premium/late-game chefs have stronger identities; tip and cost ladders smoothed. Cafe-wide bonuses (Tip Multiplier, Cook Speed) tuned carefully since they buff every cook at once.
- **Infectable zombies & staff** rebalanced across energy, attack, speed, regen, cost, and value. Underpowered toxin/cash units brought up to useful value; energy/regen reworked so far more units are viable.
- Standout identities: **Governor** (top tip specialist), **Godfather** (strong all-rounder), **Zombie Man** (elite combat monster), **Galacticus & Henchman** improved.

## 🍔 Built on the v1.0 food rebalance

Less grindy food progression · stronger premium/DLC/special-stove-chain dishes · cleaner food values · cash-to-toxin exchange adjusted for the stronger economy.
